## 🎯 Exercises

### 1. `convert_celsius_to_fahrenheit(celsius)`
Convert degrees Celsius to Fahrenheit using the formula:
$$F = C \times \frac{9}{5} + 32$$

### 2. `calculate_total_seconds(hours, minutes, seconds)`
Convert a time interval of hours, minutes, and seconds into total elapsed seconds.

### 3. `compute_sphere_volume(radius)`
Compute the volume of a sphere of radius $r$:
$$V = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3$$
*(Use $\pi \approx 3.141592653589793$ from `math.pi`)*
